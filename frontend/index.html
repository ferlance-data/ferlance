POINT-IN-TIME STATEMENT

Financial position

Choose a date to reconstruct assets, liabilities, and equity from balances entered on or before it.

Record an account balance

Enter the account balance as at the date selected above. Enter zero explicitly when the balance is zero.

What this statement needs

Record cash, bank, receivables, inventory value, fixed assets, payables, loans, and equity balances. Each account carries forward its latest recorded balance on or before the chosen date; stale balances are flagged.

Amounts must use the workspace currency. Inventory value is entered separately from unit counts. Missing categories stay visibly incomplete; zero is never assumed.

This is an operational balance-sheet view, not a tax filing or full general ledger. Verify opening balances and consult an accountant.
